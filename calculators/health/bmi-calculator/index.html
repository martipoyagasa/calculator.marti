How it’s calculated

BMI divides your weight in kilograms by your height in metres squared. Adult categories follow WHO ranges.

Formula: BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)²

Example

70 kg at 175 cm: 70 ÷ 1.75² = 22.9, a healthy weight.

Frequently asked questions

Is BMI accurate for everyone?

No. It does not distinguish muscle from fat and is only a screening tool. Talk to a health professional for advice.
