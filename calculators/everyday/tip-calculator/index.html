How it’s calculated

The tip is the bill times the tip percentage. Add it to the bill, then divide by the number of people.

Formula: Total = bill × (1 + tip%) ; Per person = Total ÷ people

Example

$80 with an 18% tip = $94.40, or $47.20 each for two.
